# Common AI Mistakes When Writing Characters and Dialogue

Artificial Intelligence can be a powerful brainstorming tool for writers, but it often falls into predictable, repetitive traps when generating characters and dialogue. Understanding these "AI-isms" will help you spot unnatural writing and breathe genuine human life into your stories.

---

## Part 1: Mistakes in Character Writing

### 1. The "Walking Trope" (Lack of Depth)
AI relies heavily on statistical averages from existing literature, resulting in flat archetypes rather than complex individuals.
* **The Mistake:** A grumpy detective who drinks too much coffee, has a failed marriage, and plays by his own rules—with zero unexpected traits.
* **The Fix:** Introduce contradictions. Give the cynical detective a secret passion for baking delicate pastries or caring for stray cats.

### 2. "Tell, Don't Show" Emotional States
AI loves to explicitly label a character’s emotions rather than letting their actions reveal how they feel.
* **The Mistake:** *"Sarah felt a surge of overwhelming grief and anger as she looked at the broken vase."*
* **The Fix:** Show physical reactions and behavior. *"Sarah stared at the shards on the floor, her jaw clenched so tightly her teeth ached, before slowly reaching for the broom."*

### 3. Over-Reliance on Melodrama and Cliché Phrases
AI frequently uses over-the-top physical descriptions to convey emotion.
* **Common AI Clichés:**
  * *"A shiver ran down [their] spine."*
  * *"Let out a breath [they] didn't realize [they] were holding."*
  * *"Eyes widened in shock / narrowed into slits."*
  * *"Knuckles turned white."*
  * *"A single tear rolled down [their] cheek."*

### 4. Instant Forgiveness and Moral Preachiness
Because AI models are trained to be helpful, harmless, and polite, their characters often resolve deep conflicts way too easily and lecture each other like therapists.
* **The Mistake:** Two bitter enemies have a two-minute conversation, realize they "aren't so different after all," and immediately become allies.
* **The Fix:** Let characters hold grudges, act irrationally, be petty, and take time to change.

### 5. Inconsistent Motivation and Memory Loss
In longer stories, AI forgets why a character acts a certain way, bending their personality to fit whatever the current scene requires.
* **The Mistake:** A fiercely cautious character suddenly walks into an obvious trap without hesitation just so the plot can move forward.

## Part 2: Mistakes in Dialogue Writing

### 1. Everyone Sounds the Same (Homogenized Voice)
Whether it is a 12-year-old street urchin, a medieval knight, or a futuristic CEO, AI characters often share the exact same cadence, vocabulary, and sentence structure.
* **The Fix:** Vary sentence length, slang, rhythm, and what characters *choose* to notice or talk about based on their background.

### 2. "As You Know, Bob" (Clumsy Exposition)
AI uses dialogue as an info-dump tool, making characters state facts they would already know just to inform the reader.
* **The Mistake:** *"As you know, brother, ever since our father died three years ago in the Great War, I've been running the family blacksmith shop alone."*
* **The Fix:** Let information leak naturally through conflict or subtext. *"Dad's forge is falling apart, and I'm the only one keeping the fire lit."*

### 3. Zero Subtext (Characters Say Exactly What They Mean)
Real people rarely state their exact feelings or intentions outright; they deflect, lie, use sarcasm, or change the subject. AI characters are brutally, unnaturally literal.
* **The Mistake:** *"I am angry at you because you forgot my birthday and it makes me feel unappreciated."*
* **The Fix:** Use subtext. *"Oh, don't worry about it. Tuesday was just a regular day anyway. Did you finish your report?"*

### 4. Overuse of Names in Conversation
People rarely say the other person's name out loud during a one-on-one conversation unless they are angry or trying to get their attention. AI uses names constantly.
* **The Mistake:** *"John, I don't think this is a good idea."* / *"Well, Mary, we don't have a choice, Mary."*
* **The Fix:** Cut 90% of direct name mentions in dialogue.

### 5. Repetitive Dialogue Tags and Adverb Abuse
AI struggles to let dialogue stand on its own, propping up every line with unnecessary tags and adverbs.
* **The Mistake:** *"I can't believe you did that," she said angrily. "It wasn't my fault," he replied defensively. "Stop lying!" she shouted furiously.*
* **The Fix:** Use simple *"said"* or action beats instead of adverbs. *"I can't believe you did that." She slammed the folder onto the desk.*

### 6. Robotic Ping-Pong Pacing
AI dialogue often follows a strict, monotonous rhythm: Character A speaks two sentences, Character B speaks two sentences, repeat. There are no interruptions, trailing thoughts, or awkward silences.
* **The Fix:** Have characters interrupt each other, ignore questions, trail off mid-sentence, or respond with silence and body language.

### 7. Overly Formal or "Therapy-Speak" Phrasing
AI often strips away contractions and injects modern corporate or psychological buzzwords into settings where they don't belong.
* **The Mistake:** A fantasy warrior saying, *"I need you to respect my boundaries and validate my feelings right now."*
* **The Fix:** Match vocabulary to the setting and use natural contractions (*don't, can't, we're*).

## Quick Checklist for Editing AI-Generated Characters & Dialogue

- [ ] **The Name Test:** If you cover the dialogue tags, can you tell which character is speaking just by their word choice?
- [ ] **The Subtext Check:** Are characters saying exactly what they feel, or are they hiding behind subtext?
- [ ] **Cliché Sweep:** Did you delete "breaths they didn't know they were holding" and "shivers down spines"?
- [ ] **Name Trimming:** Did you remove unnecessary uses of characters saying each other's names?
- [ ] **Flaw Check:** Is the character allowed to be selfish, irrational, or wrong without immediately apologizing?
